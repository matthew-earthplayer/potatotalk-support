常見問題

為什麼 PotatoTalk 需要麥克風權限？

PotatoTalk 的口說練習會使用麥克風錄下你的練習內容，讓你完成跟讀與口說流程。依目前 V1 production 版本的資料流，錄音只在裝置本機處理，不會上傳到 PotatoTalk 伺服器。

我的學習進度會上傳嗎？

依目前 V1 production 版本，學習進度、設定、My Library／Review 等資料儲存在你的裝置上，沒有雲端同步。

如何移除 PotatoTalk 的本機資料？

PotatoTalk 沒有使用者帳號。你可以使用 App 內可用的重置／清除功能處理部分資料；若要移除 App 儲存在裝置上的資料，可刪除 App。

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does PotatoTalk request microphone access?

PotatoTalk uses the microphone for speaking practice. Based on the current V1 production data flow, practice recordings are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to a PotatoTalk server.

Is my learning progress uploaded?

In the current V1 production version, learning progress, settings, and My Library / Review data are stored locally on your device. There is no cloud sync.

How can I remove local PotatoTalk data?

PotatoTalk does not use user accounts. You can use available in-app reset or clear functions for certain data. To remove app data stored on the device, you can delete the app.
